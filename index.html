    Orders of the Day HOW TO PLAY

    1. Deploy. Click cells on the Devin board to place Carrier (5), Battleship (4), Cruiser (3), Submarine (3), Destroyer (2). R rotates; Randomize deploys the whole fleet at once.
    2. Engage. Fire by clicking a square on the Claude Code tracking grid. Red peg = hit, white peg = miss.
    3. Return fire. Claude Code hunts at random until it draws blood, then chases adjacent squares until the ship goes down.
    4. Victory. Sink all five enemy ships before your own fleet is lost.
